ABOUT VITAGIT

A homebrew catalog,
built around the community.

VitaGit is a convenient way to discover PlayStation Vita homebrew projects and find their original download sources. The site is a catalog and presentation layer; the projects themselves remain with their respective developers and repositories.

What VitaGit does

VitaGit collects publicly available project information so users can browse apps, games, ports, emulators and utilities in one place. When a download is available, the site points you toward the project's published GitHub release or project page rather than hosting the software itself.

Downloads and legal use

VitaGit is not a piracy service and does not intentionally distribute commercial games, copyrighted ROMs, pirated content, or material that has not been legitimately provided by its author. A listing does not mean VitaGit owns, created, endorses, or redistributes the underlying project.

Please use each project according to its own license and the laws applicable to you. Only download and use software and content that you are legally entitled to use.

Project ownership

Homebrew software, icons, names, trademarks and other assets remain with their respective authors and rights holders. If you are a developer and something about your project is incorrect, outdated, or should not be listed, please contact the maintainers or open an issue in the database repository.

Database source

The catalog is built from the VitaGit database and its upstream catalog data. Metadata can change as projects are updated, renamed, moved or discontinued.
